# 03 — Physical Marketing Pack (Brochure / Pamphlet / Poster)

## A) Tri-fold brochure structure

### Front cover
- Headline: **"Complete School Management, Simplified"**
- Sub-line: Admissions • Attendance • Fees • Exams • Parent Communication
- CTA: **Book Free Demo** (phone + email + QR code)

### Inside panel 1: Why schools switch
- Reduce admin workload and paperwork
- Real-time visibility for principal and management
- Better parent communication and trust
- Centralized records for academics and finance

### Inside panel 2: Core modules
- Admissions and student lifecycle
- Staff management and attendance
- Class/subject/timetable planning
- Fee invoices, collections, and receipts
- Exams, marks entry, and report outputs
- Transport, inventory, accounting, and audit logs

### Inside panel 3: Parent experience
- Attendance alerts
- Fee and receipt visibility
- Homework and exam updates
- WhatsApp and notification-based communication

### Back panel
- Implementation promise: onboarding + training + support
- Deployment options: web + mobile-friendly access
- CTA buttons/text:
  - "Schedule School Demo"
  - "Start 60-Day Pilot"

## D) Distribution plan
- Radius targeting: schools within 30-50 km clusters
- Weekly target: 100-150 pamphlets + 30 brochures to decision-makers
- Priority places:
  - School admin offices
  - Education consultant offices
  - Bookstores/uniform suppliers near school hubs
  - District-level education events

## E) Physical campaign KPIs
- Brochures handed to decision-maker count
- Inbound calls/WhatsApp from print collateral
- QR scan count per collateral type
- School meetings booked from field visits
