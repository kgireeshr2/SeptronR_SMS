## Design standards for premium look (important)

- Use a clean white base with 1 primary and 1 accent brand color only
- Keep generous spacing; avoid text-heavy blocks
- Use real product screenshots, not generic stock UI
- Use high-resolution icons with one consistent style
- Keep heading hierarchy clear and bold
- Ensure print-safe margins and 3 mm bleed
- Use a professional QR code with short URL

---

4. Exam screenshot: marks/results
