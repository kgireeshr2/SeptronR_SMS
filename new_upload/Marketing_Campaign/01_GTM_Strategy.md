# 01 — Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1) Product positioning
**Positioning statement**
A modern, multi-tenant School Management System for K-12 schools that unifies admissions, academics, fees, attendance, exams, staff operations, transport, inventory, accounting, and parent communication in one platform.

## 2) Core value pillars (based on your implemented modules)
1. **Academic Operations**: classes, subjects, timetable, attendance, exams, homework, calendar
2. **Administrative Control**: admissions, student/staff records, roles and permissions, audit logs
3. **Financial Visibility**: fee structures, invoices, payments, accounting, payroll support
4. **Parent Communication**: announcements, notifications, WhatsApp integration, reports
5. **Scalable Multi-School Setup**: multi-tenant architecture with role-based access

## 3) Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Private schools (K-12), 500 to 3000 students
- School groups operating multiple campuses
- Budget-sensitive schools currently using spreadsheets + WhatsApp + manual accounting
- Decision makers:
  - Owner/Trustee (cost, growth)
  - Principal (academic governance)
  - Administrator/Operations manager (daily execution)
  - Accountant (fees and reconciliation)

## 4) Key pains to solve
- Disconnected systems for attendance, fee collection, and communication
- Manual exam result processing and delayed report publishing
- Parent complaints due to poor visibility on fees, attendance, and homework
- No leadership-level dashboard for operations and financial health
- Lack of role-based control and auditability

## 5) Messaging framework
### Primary message
"Run your entire school from one platform — admissions to report cards — with real-time parent communication."

### Benefit-led messages
- Save admin time by automating attendance, fees, and exam workflows
- Improve fee collection follow-up through invoice and payment tracking
- Give parents instant updates through app, email, and WhatsApp
- Make school operations transparent with audit logs and role-based workflows

### Proof themes for campaigns
- End-to-end modules in one system (not just attendance or fees)
- Multi-tenant and role-based architecture
- API-first backend with mobile + web support
- Works for both single schools and school groups

## 6) Offer strategy
- **Starter pilot (60 days)**: implementation + onboarding + staff training
- **Annual plan**: discounted yearly contract for schools that convert after pilot
- **Referral offer**: existing client introduces another school and gets service credits

## 7) Channel strategy (Digital + Physical)
### Digital channels
- Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram): principal/admin targeting
- Google Search Ads: high-intent keywords (school ERP, school management software)
- LinkedIn outreach: school chains, trustees, education directors
- YouTube demo clips: 60–120 second feature explainers
- WhatsApp nurture: brochure + demo links + reminders
- Email drip: role-based sequences (principal vs accountant)

### Physical channels
- Printed brochures and one-page pamphlets for schools
- Posters for education expos and local school-owner associations
- Principal roundtable/demo day events
- Partner network (education consultants, computer labs, local IT vendors)

## 8) Funnel model
1. **Awareness**: social ads + search + posters + local events
2. **Interest**: landing page + case-style demo videos + brochure download
3. **Consideration**: live demo + ROI worksheet + role-specific Q&A
4. **Conversion**: pilot proposal + implementation timeline + contract
5. **Retention**: onboarding success reviews + referrals

## 9) Sales narrative (demo flow)
1. Admissions + student onboarding
2. Attendance + timetable + homework
3. Fees + receipts + accounting view
4. Exams + marks + result publishing
5. Parent communication + WhatsApp updates
6. Dashboard + audit + permissions

## 10) Success metrics
- Marketing qualified leads (MQLs)
- Demo booked rate
- Demo-to-pilot conversion
- Pilot-to-annual conversion
- Cost per lead (CPL)
- 60-day revenue from new schools
