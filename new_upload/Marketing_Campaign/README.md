# SMS Marketing Campaign Pack (2026)

This folder contains a practical promotion plan for your School Management System (SMS) across digital and physical channels.

## Files
- `01_GTM_Strategy.md` — market positioning, ICP, messaging, channel strategy
- `02_90_Day_Campaign_Plan.md` — phased execution plan (awareness → demo → conversion)
- `03_Physical_Marketing_Pack.md` — brochure/pamphlet/poster/event booth content plan
- `04_Content_Calendar_8_Weeks.md` — ready publishing calendar for social + email + WhatsApp
- `05_Budget_KPI_Tracker_Template.csv` — budget and KPI tracking template

## Suggested usage order
1. Finalize your target segment and pricing story in `01_GTM_Strategy.md`
2. Start execution week-by-week from `02_90_Day_Campaign_Plan.md`
3. Send collateral brief to designer/print vendor from `03_Physical_Marketing_Pack.md`
4. Execute weekly content using `04_Content_Calendar_8_Weeks.md`
5. Track performance in `05_Budget_KPI_Tracker_Template.csv`

## Campaign goal
Generate qualified school leads and convert them into paid pilots/annual subscriptions by highlighting operational outcomes:
- Faster admissions and onboarding
- Better fee collection visibility
- Accurate attendance and exam workflows
- Parent communication via app + WhatsApp + email
- Single platform for academics + operations + finance
