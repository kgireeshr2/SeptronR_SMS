# 04 — Digital Content Calendar (8 Weeks)

## Channel mix
- LinkedIn: leadership and trust content
- Facebook/Instagram: awareness and engagement
- YouTube: short product walkthroughs
- Email: nurture and webinar invites
- WhatsApp: reminders and demo follow-ups

## Weekly publishing rhythm
- Mon: pain-point post
- Tue: feature spotlight
- Wed: short demo clip
- Thu: testimonial/case narrative
- Fri: CTA post (book demo)
- Sat: educational tip or webinar snippet
