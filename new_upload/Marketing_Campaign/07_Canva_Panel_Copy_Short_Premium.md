## Quick Canva Placement Guide (for fast production)

- Use VERSION A if design is visual-heavy with big screenshots.
- Use VERSION B if targeting principal/owner-level decision makers.
- Keep heading text in bold and body copy in short blocks.
- Use one CTA button style consistently on front and back panels.
- Place website and phone number on at least 3 panels (Front, Flap, Back).
- Add QR code on back panel linking to contact form or WhatsApp chat.
