# 02 — 90-Day Campaign Plan

## Campaign objective
Build a predictable lead pipeline and close pilot contracts with schools using a blended digital + on-ground strategy.

## Targets (90 days)
- 120-180 qualified leads
- 40-60 demos
- 10-15 pilot signups
- 4-8 annual contract conversions

---

## Phase 1 (Days 1-30): Foundation + Awareness
### Week 1
- Finalize ICP, pricing sheets, and pitch deck
- Create campaign landing page with demo CTA
- Prepare brochure, pamphlet, and poster print files
- Set up CRM pipeline stages (Lead → Qualified → Demo → Proposal → Closed)

### Week 2
- Launch Meta + Google Search ads
- Publish 3 intro posts (problem-led, feature-led, trust-led)
- Launch first email campaign to local school database
- Print first batch: 200 brochures + 500 pamphlets + 20 posters

### Week 3
- Conduct cold outreach: 15-20 schools/day
- Distribute brochures to target school clusters
- Run WhatsApp prospecting campaign (with consent-based opt-in)
- Publish short product demo video (attendance + fees workflow)

### Week 4
- Host 1 live webinar: "How schools reduce admin load with unified ERP"
- Retarget ad audiences who visited landing page
- Book 10-15 demo meetings for next phase

---

## Phase 2 (Days 31-60): Consideration + Demo Conversion
### Week 5
- Run role-based demos:
  - Principal view
  - Admin operations view
  - Accounts/fee collection view
- Share 2-page implementation roadmap with every demo prospect

### Week 6
- Launch case-story campaign (before/after workflows)
- Send email drip sequence (3 emails in 10 days)
- Start referral outreach through existing contacts

### Week 7
- Organize local in-person roundtable (10-20 decision makers)
- Hand out brochures + one-page ROI sheet
- Offer limited-time pilot discount (valid 15 days)

### Week 8
- Proposal sprint week:
  - Follow-up calls to all demo accounts
  - Proposal submission within 24h after each qualified demo
  - Objection handling FAQ for pricing, onboarding, and support

---

## Phase 3 (Days 61-90): Closing + Expansion
### Week 9
- Push conversion campaign: "Go live before new term"
- Launch testimonial-style creatives (even pilot testimonials)
- Run remarketing ad set focused on booked-demo audience

### Week 10
- Close open proposals with deadline-based incentives
- Begin onboarding for signed pilots
- Capture implementation milestones as future proof content

### Week 11
- Upsell annual contracts to active pilots showing early outcomes
- Ask for 1 referral per satisfied pilot school
- Start partner acquisition (consultants/system integrators)

### Week 12
- Review campaign metrics and channel ROI
- Double down on top-2 channels by conversion
- Publish quarter-end performance deck and next-quarter forecast

---

## Weekly operating cadence
- Monday: campaign planning + pipeline review
- Tuesday-Thursday: outbound, demos, follow-ups
- Friday: reporting + creative optimization + next-week prep
- Saturday (optional): school visit slots / principal workshops

## Team responsibilities
- Founder/Sales lead: demos, proposals, closing
- Marketing lead: ads, content, email, WhatsApp campaigns
- Field executive: brochure distribution, event coordination
- Product specialist: onboarding and implementation confidence

## Risk controls
- Low lead quality → tighten targeting + improve landing page qualification form
- High demo no-show rate → WhatsApp reminders at T-24h and T-1h
- Slow proposal closures → standard proposal templates + expiry windows
- Long implementation fears → show 2-week onboarding plan
